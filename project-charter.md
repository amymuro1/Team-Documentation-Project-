# Project Charter

## Our Mission

Our project, "A Guide to Non-Profit Grant Writing," is a guide in which we seek to create a baseline for users when entering the grant application process. Our focus is towards nonprofits due to the evolving need for funding while seeking resources alternative to a traditional grant writer. As a reference organization, we chose Flying B Animal Rescue & Sanctuary located in Ennis, TX. Our team consists of three members who have experience in grant application and two who will provide a fresh, inexperienced perspective to readers who are less familiar with the process. In enabling the guide to have varying perspectives, our non-exposed members will provide real-time, user testing perspective during the drafting process. We will not be directly working with our target organization, rather using the "client" as a point of reference. Our intended users include those interested in learning about grant writing, non-profits that may need the information to initiate the grant application process given the need for external funding, or first-time users that may be taking on the role of applying for their personal organization such as an employee or volunteer. The potential stakeholders for this project include potential investors, owners, a board of directors, employees, and volunteers. We intend to create a printable, digital guide designed through a documentation design platform (likely Adobe InDesign) for new users to have a framework to follow. A design mockup will be incorporated to show the process of creating the documentation. To show all the processes in a conclusive manner, we intend to create a presentation that incorporates the steps of the process and provides a visual guide through the creation of the documentation and later application of the workflow. 

## Purpose Statement

The purpose of this repository is to document the collection and distribution of information as it relates to the ***A Guide to Non-Profit Grant Writing***. It is used to help keep group members informed on the progression of the project and allow for easy review of workflow. We intend to make information for grant writing a more accessible media to best support groups that require grant funding.

## Scope Statement

The intended scope of this project is to create an introductory guide to grant writing that can be used by non-profits, entry-level grant writers, or anyone making proposals related to accumulating funds from a granting organization. The document is by no means exhaustive to every situation but does create a framework.
