# Executive Summary 

"Through the review and application of an introductory digital grant application resource, we seek to address the level of entry for nonprofits seeking funding for their organizations. 
Nonprofits may have limited access to specialists in the grant writing field and can face lack of funding application options. Our goals in this study are to review user sentiments towards 
the application process, observe how they apply their own information hierarchy to the concepts provided, and to distill textual feedback into actionable insights. In creating this study, 
we sought to collect users with and without grant writing experience to aggregate data from both the informed and uninformed perspective. Feedback on the efficacy of our terminology alongside 
language from our experienced users inform our research questions further. In gaining perspective from the inexperienced users, we can see the applicability of our information architecture. 
Users are presented with a reference grant application resource alongside supplementary instructional material to scaffold understanding. A Google Form providing feedback boxes allows for users 
to respond to presented scenarios to assess comprehension and application rate in relation to supplementary material."
