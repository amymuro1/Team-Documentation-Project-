# Guide to Grant Writing Style Sheet
## Voice & Tone
-	Maintain a professional, and helpful tone throughout the guide.
- Explain all jargon and acronyms. This guide is for a general audience.
- Stick to the active voice and present tense.
-	First person plural ("we/our") when speaking as the guide writers.
## Names & Identities
- Don't abbreviate names on first use.
## Numbers, Statistics, and Dates
- Spell out one through nine; use numerals for values greater than 10.
- Always use numerals with units, percentages, and money.
- Never start a sentence with a numeral.
- Use full format dates (i.e September 18, 2026).
 ## Terminology
  - Use consistent terminology throughout guide.
  - Maintain a running terminology glossary in guide.
## Formatting
- Use 18 point Open Sans Bold for headings.
- Use 14 point Open Sans Bold for subheadings.
- Use 12 point Open Sans regular for body paragraphs.
- Document margins are 1 inch.
- Single line spacing
- Use standard circle bullet points for bulleted lists.
## Citations & Sources
- Cite all other guides used as a source or model.
- Sources should be no more than 3-5 years old.
