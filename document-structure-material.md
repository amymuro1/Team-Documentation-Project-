# Nonprofit Grant Funding Application Guide 

## Base Documentation

### ***Note to Reader:*** ###

Welcome to your first steps in learning to apply for grant application. ***We are glad you are here!*** Below we have provided a short description of some preliminary notes to review prior to starting an application:

#### The following document is an overview of the common major components of a grant application. 
It is important to note that many organizations may require a Letter of Interest or Letter of Inquiry (both use the acronym LOI) to gauge your readiness for grant application or if your needs match what the granting organization can provide. 

It is crucial to review any preliminary instructions provided by the granting organization to which you wish 
to apply prior to grant application. When approaching a grant application, compiling the information provided in this document allows for ease of use that can be integrated into your grant application. Grants, like book 
genres, have a variety of instances where they fit a specific situation. For example, granting organizations may integrate their own software that requires this same information but to be manually entered into their system. 
This document will provide the steps for a traditional grant documentation format.
