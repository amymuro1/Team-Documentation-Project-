# Team Audience and Task Research Plan

## Research Purpose Statement

The purpose of our grant documentation research is to improve the accessibility of grant funding and associated materials to a wider audience. The extent of our research expands beyond creating a usable document but rather extends the knowledge base to a greater whole. *Without interrogating how our methods occur, we would not truly understand the needs and goals of our intended audience.*

## Method Description

To complete this project, A Guide to Non-Profit Writing, our group will be conducting background literature review. Using GitHub as a repository, each member of our group will contribute previously published material; such as previously written grants, previously written guides, and application portals for existing grants. The material will be evaluated for practical application towards our reference partner, Flying B Animal Rescue & Sanctuary (Ennis, TX). We will identify areas for improvement on existing materials and areas that can be clarified with the goal of a guide for novice users.

## Participant and Data Use Plan
We will evaluate the guide by conducting usability testing on a draft version of the document. We will ask other students in class (ones not in our documentation group) to do a short survey about the guide. We will ask for their consent to do the survey, and their responses to the usability survey will be confidential and not used outside of the project. 

## Usability Test Protocol
The usability test will conduct of asking participants to perform a task related to the guide (task TBD but likely involving using the content in the guide) and how they feel about the guide's content and formatting. We will also ask participant's experience level with grant writing, as well as basic demographic information.

## Ethics and Consent Considerations
In creating an ethical documentation process, we will create an informed consent document similar to the university’s standard IRB protocol. The participant risk and conflict of interest are minimal to non-existent. Participation will be presented as non-mandatory and we will ask for consent to use responses for data purposes by maintaining anonymity for participants. We must consider that not all potential users may choose to participate. We must consider our approach to a low user feedback pool. In the event of these situations, we must use our best judgement to maintain the integrity of our documents and to provide the most authentic experience we can within the constraints of time and user availability.
